# Preventing the Next Pandemic: Epidemiological Compartmental Models, the Reproduction Number, and Determining Intervention Strategies

After the COVID-19 pandemic upended daily life in 2020, questions about how disease control agencies around the world could better prepare for the spread diseases arose. Epidemiol- ogy is the study of the spread of diseases, and in this paper, we explore how epidemiological models can inform disease inter- vention strategies. In particular, we show how epidemiological systems of ordinary di!erential equations (ODEs) can project the long-term trajectories of diseases, along with how along small changes in assumptions can result in large impacts on the model. Additionally, we discover the importance of the reproduction number R0 in determining long-term behavior of a disease while also exploring how models can be manipu- lated to uncover key insights that inform disease intervention, such as designing quarantining policy or setting vaccination thresholds. Studying the spread of disease through these mod- els and methods can contribute to disease mitigation in the long-run
